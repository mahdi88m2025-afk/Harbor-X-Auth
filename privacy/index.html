HARBOR X · SPLASH

Trakt integration privacy

Published 1 November 2026 · Independent connector in Development

Scope and operator

Harbor X is an independent Windows desktop media application maintained by Splash (GitHub: mahdi88m2025-afk), based on Harbor. This notice covers the independently registered Trakt connector currently in local Development, its proposed HTTPS callback, and the related data flows described below. It is published on 1 November 2026. It does not claim that this connector is already included in the older public v0.9.26 release, or that Trakt has enabled Production registration. Other integrations have their own providers and privacy terms. Harbor X uses Trakt but is not endorsed or certified by Trakt.

What connecting does

Connecting is optional. You sign in and approve access on Trakt's official website; Harbor X does not ask for your Trakt password. The desktop connector uses OAuth Authorization Code with PKCE S256 and a per-attempt state check, without embedding a Client Secret. The app receives access and refresh tokens, an expiry and creation time, and your Trakt username. Connecting enables account-linked features, including playback reporting where the app can identify the movie or episode. To stop account-linked reporting and access on this installation, disconnect Trakt. Pause reporting follows the app's playback/list-status preference; not every reporting function has a separate switch.

Data and purposes

For the features you use, the connector requests your profile identity and avatar, watchlist entries, watched-history entries, recommendations and upcoming viewing items. Entries can contain title, year, movie/show/episode identifiers, list position or date, watched date, season and episode numbers. Playback reports send the identified title/episode, start/pause/stop event and progress to Trakt; a stop event can mark an item watched. Watchlist changes send selected item identifiers. If you use the comments and ratings features, Trakt receives the text, spoiler/review flags, likes or rating and item identifiers you explicitly submit. Displayed comments include their public authors, text, dates, spoiler flags, rating and reply/like counts. No account-wide list, post or rating is published by this privacy page.

Where credentials and data are kept

The new connector stores its grant on your Windows device using current-user Windows DPAPI, separated by provider, Harbor X profile and registered Client ID. It also holds a working copy in application memory while running. DPAPI protects the saved grant; it does not protect against every program already running as your Windows user. Other settings, metadata, local watchlists, avatar preferences, comment/rating caches and browser data are not all encrypted by this mechanism. The publisher has no token-exchange server for this connector and does not receive its tokens as part of the designed connection flow. This statement is limited to this connector, not a claim of a full network audit of every Harbor X feature.

Retention

The current grant remains until you disconnect, replace it by reconnecting, or remove the relevant application data. Expiry ends the access token's validity but does not automatically erase the stored record; the app can refresh a valid grant. A revoked or invalid grant stops automatic renewal when the provider reports it invalid. In-memory lists and history are used for the current view/session and are replaced or cleared as views and connection state change. Locally saved ratings and copies of comments you post have no automatic expiry in the reviewed code. Imported bookmarks, saved preferences and an avatar you choose can remain until you remove or change them. WebView/HTTP caches follow their normal cache management and may outlive a session. These categories do not have one universal retention period. Uninstalling normally preserves user data unless you explicitly select its removal.

Disconnect, revoke and delete

In Settings → Account → Trakt, choose Disconnect from Trakt and confirm. The new connector replaces the current local protected grant with an encrypted null record and stops scheduling account-authenticated work; the updated Development code cancels queued work whose authorization changed and discards stale responses. A request already transmitted may finish at Trakt. Disconnecting is not guaranteed forensic erasure and does not delete Trakt's server-side history, comments, ratings or watchlist. Also remove Harbor X in Trakt's connected-app settings to revoke provider-side access, including grants on other installations. Manage Trakt-held records and any provider deletion request directly through Trakt. To remove local imported bookmarks, remove them in the relevant library/watchlist; to remove an adopted avatar, change your profile/avatar preference. For complete local removal, close Harbor X and remove only the data for the installation/profile you intend to erase, plus any backups you control. Ask for help identifying that profile before deleting it; Development, Testing and your daily installation use separate locations. The maintainer cannot remotely erase files on your computer, your other devices, or GitHub/Trakt logs.

Backups and older data

The new DPAPI grant is outside the ordinary portable settings backup and is not exported by that backup builder. Older settings/session records are deliberately not automatically migrated into the new connector or erased. Older backups and some settings may therefore contain legacy account credentials, service keys, library data or private addon URLs. Treat every backup as private, even after disconnecting, and remove copies you no longer want separately. Reinstalling or restoring older data can restore retained data; authorizing the new connector remains a separate action.

Other recipients and optional transfers

Requests leave your device for Trakt and its infrastructure over HTTPS; the service receives the network information needed to process them, such as your IP address and request details. To display artwork or enrich a title, Harbor X can request its identifiers from TMDB or Cinemeta and fetch their images; this can reveal which titles are being requested. If you explicitly confirm Import in Move your watchlist while signed in to Stremio, selected Trakt item identifiers, titles and types are copied into that Stremio account's library. Export does the reverse for the selected watchlist entries. These are user-directed copies, not a promise that disconnecting Trakt will remove the copy from Stremio. Other enabled media sources, addons, account integrations and GitHub updates have separate network/data flows. This notice does not promise that the whole application is offline or anonymous. Harbor X does not operate a Trakt catalog-resale, credential-sharing or dataset feed as part of this integration.

The public callback website

The proposed Trakt return address is https://mahdi88m2025-afk.github.io/Harbor-X-Auth/trakt/. GitHub Pages hosts it. The initial request can disclose your IP address, browser request information and OAuth code/state query parameters to GitHub; the PKCE verifier stays in the desktop app. The page has no password form, token exchange, analytics, third-party scripts/assets, service worker or persistent browser storage. It removes the query from the visible address and temporarily holds a response in memory for transfer back to the desktop app. It clears that copy after a successful copy, on its clear action, on navigation, or after five minutes. Your browser, extensions, synchronization, OS clipboard/history and GitHub logs can retain information beyond the page's control. Do not paste an authorization response into email, GitHub issues or chat. This privacy page itself is static text with no script, tracking form or account login.

Privacy questions and requests

Contact Splash through a minimal issue in the Harbor-X-Auth GitHub repository. Issues are public: do not post your email address, account name, watch history, private URLs, passwords, access/refresh tokens, callback response, backups or logs. You can request local deletion instructions without supplying those details. If a request genuinely needs private information, ask for a private communication method first; do not publish it in an issue. Any information you voluntarily send through GitHub is processed and retained there under GitHub's privacy policy and the issue's retention. This channel does not give the maintainer access to your local grant or authority to erase Trakt's own records.

Updates to this notice

We will update the public notice when the connector's material data practices change. The review behind this notice checked source code and existing test evidence; it does not claim that pending real write-sync, refresh-rotation or revocation tests have passed. Changes to Harbor X's released application require a separate release decision.
